Your first SIP playbook

A simple plan for starting monthly investing in your early 20s: which funds to pick, a few stocks and ETFs, smallcases, and how to set it all up on Groww.

Ground rules

  • Always pick the Direct plan with the Growth option. Fees are lower.
  • 3–4 funds is enough. More funds usually means owning the same stocks twice.
  • Keep 3–6 months of expenses in an FD or liquid fund before going big on equity.
  • Don't stop your SIP when markets fall. You're buying more units for the same money.
  • Check once or twice a year, not every day. Increase the SIP when your salary goes up.

Stock SIP and ETFs

Groww's Stock SIP buys a stock or ETF for you automatically every week or month, from ₹500 a time (or a fixed number of shares). You need a Groww demat account and Autopay. Brokerage applies to each purchase.

Keep this to 10–20% of what you invest. Single stocks swing more than funds. ETFs are the safer way to use Stock SIP.

These are well-known large companies to learn with, not buy tips. Check the current price and news on Groww first.

Smallcases

A smallcase is a ready-made basket of stocks or ETFs built around an idea. You buy the actual shares into your own demat account in one go.

On Groww: open smallcases.groww.in, or the smallcase app and choose "Login with Groww". On Zerodha it's built into Kite. If you don't see it in the app, use that website.

Each stock in a basket costs brokerage, plus rebalancing costs. Under about ₹5,000 a month, plain mutual fund SIPs are cheaper and simpler. Fees and availability change, so check the smallcase page before buying.

Setting it up on Groww

One-time setup
  1. Download Groww, sign up and finish KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, selfie and signature.
  2. Turn on Stocks (demat) too if you want Stock SIP, ETFs or smallcases. Mutual funds alone don't need it.
  3. Set up Autopay (e-mandate or UPI). Keep the limit above your SIP, e.g. ₹10,000 for a ₹5,000 SIP, so payments aren't rejected.
Mutual fund SIP
  1. Search the fund name. Check it says Direct and Growth.
  2. Tap Start SIP, enter the amount, and pick a date a few days after salary arrives (e.g. the 5th).
  3. Repeat for each fund. Turn on Step-up SIP (e.g. +10% a year) if it's offered.
Stock or ETF SIP
  1. Open the stock or ETF page (e.g. NIFTYBEES), tap Create Stock SIP, choose amount or quantity, weekly or monthly, then confirm Autopay.
After that
  1. Review every 6–12 months. Don't sell in a panic when markets drop.
  2. Tax on selling equity: held over 1 year, 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh a year; under 1 year, 20%. Check current rules when you sell.
